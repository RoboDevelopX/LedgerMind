Your money, remembered. Your data, kept.

Ledgermind is a private finance assistant. Bring a bank statement as CSV, ask questions in plain English, and it remembers your goals, budgets and habits between conversations. All figures are computed on your machine; the model only ever sees summaries.

  • CSV only, by design. No bank logins, no account aggregators, nothing that can move money.
  • Aggregates, not ledgers. Transaction rows, account numbers and raw descriptions stay local.
  • NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory. Nano, Super and Ultra, each for the job it suits.
